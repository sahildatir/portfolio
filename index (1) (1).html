// About me

Where the shop floor meets the system.

I'm a Mechanical Engineer and SAP PP/MM Analyst at Schneider Electric, covering the full Production Execution landscape in SAP ERP — from BOM, routing and work centers to production orders, MRP, capacity planning and scheduling.

My work starts with people: gathering requirements from production, planning, SCM and quality teams, mapping AS-IS / TO-BE processes and closing gaps. It ends with a smooth go-live — data migration, cutover, UAT, hypercare and training users until the new way of working sticks.

Along the way I've helped reduce production lead time by 15–20% and automated reporting with Excel VBA and Power Automate to free planners for real decisions.
